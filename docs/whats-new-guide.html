What’s New: Departments & Director Teams

Two changes went live. This guide explains both in plain words, using your own team as the example.

1The two changes, in one line each

ChangeBeforeNow
A person’s department A person could be in only one department. A person can be in as many as you want. One is their main one.
What a Director sees A Director saw everybody in their department, automatically. A Director sees only the people you give them. Nobody else.
The one idea behind both changes Being in a department no longer decides who sees you. You decide, by handing each person to a Director by name. The department just decides who is allowed to be handed over.

2Change one: a person can be in more than one department

Some people work for two teams. Before, you had to pick one and the other team lost them. Now you tick both.

Every person has one main department (we call it their home) and any number of extra ones.

3Change two: a Director sees only the people you give them

This is the bigger change. Read this part slowly.

BeforeNow
Who a Director sees Everyone in their department. It happened by itself. Only the names you tick for them.
Two Directors in one department Both saw the same everybody. Each has their own list. They can be completely different.
A Director with no list Could not happen. Sees only themselves. This is allowed, so watch for it.
Founders Saw everyone. Still see everyone. Nothing changed for them.

How the two changes work together

Being in a department does not show you to that department’s Director. It only makes you allowed to be picked by them. Two steps, always:

  1. Step one — make them allowed. Add the department to the person. This alone shows them to nobody.
  2. Step two — hand them over. Open the Director, tick the person, press Save. Now the Director can see them.

One more rule worth knowing

A Director does not automatically see the staff under a Manager on their list. For example, if you give a Manager to a Director, the Manager’s own team stays hidden from that Director. If you want them visible, tick them too.

6How to do the two common jobs

Put a person in a second department

  1. Go to Team and press Manage on the person.
  2. Find Multi department. Tick the department you want to add.
  3. Press Save departments. A grey tag now shows on their card.

Give a person to a Director

  1. Go to Team and press Manage on the Director.
  2. Find Direct reports. Everyone allowed for that Director is listed. Anyone from another department shows a small grey tag naming their home one.
  3. Tick the people. Press Save direct reports.
If you want to remove a department from someone The system will stop you if that person is currently given to a Director because of it. Take them off the Director’s list first, then remove the department. This is on purpose — cutting a reporting line should be a decision, not an accident.

7In short

QuestionAnswer
Can one person be in two departments?Yes. One home, any number of extras.
Does adding a department show them to anyone?No. It only makes them pickable.
So what shows them to a Director?You ticking their name on that Director’s list.
Can two Directors share a person?No. One person, one Director.
Can a Director see their whole department?Only if you tick everyone in it.
Did anything change for the Founders?No. They still see and control everything.
