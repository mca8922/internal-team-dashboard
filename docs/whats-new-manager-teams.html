What’s New: Manager Teams Across Departments

A small fix you tested yourself. This explains what it does and why, using your own real example.

1The one-line version

BeforeNow
Who a Manager can add to their team Only people whose main department matched the Manager’s. Anyone with the Manager’s department anywhere on their list — main or extra.
Why this was needed We had already made this work for Directors picking their staff. A Manager picking their team worked the old way still — you spotted the mismatch while testing and asked for it to match.

2Your own test, step by step

You already ran this exact test on the live system. Here is what happened, written out plainly.

StepWhat happened
Start Vaibhav Shitap’s main department is Audit. He also has + General and + GST on his list.
The manager Test is the Manager of General. Before this fix, Vaibhav could never appear on Test’s team — his main department is Audit, not General.
The fix Test opened their team list. Vaibhav now appeared, with a small grey Audit tag showing his real main department.
Result Test ticked Vaibhav and saved. Vaibhav Shitap now reports to Test. This is confirmed in the system right now.
Confirmed working Vaibhav Shitap currently reports to both Test (his Manager, for day-to-day team work) and Dilip Pandey (his Director, for Audit). Both lines exist at the same time and don’t interfere with each other.

3Two things stayed exactly the same

QuestionAnswer
Does this let a Manager see anyone new? Only the people they actually tick. Adding a department to someone does not show them to anyone by itself — same rule as before.
Can two Managers share the same person? No. One person answers to one Manager at a time, same as before.

How to do it yourself

  1. Go to Team and press Manage on the Manager.
  2. Find Team in <department>. Anyone belonging to that department — main or extra — is listed. Anyone pulled in from another main department shows a small grey tag naming it.
  3. Tick the people. Press Save team.
