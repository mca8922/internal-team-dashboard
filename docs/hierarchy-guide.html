Team Hierarchy — How It Works

Who sits where, who can change what, and how to set it up.
Worked through with Rajesh Bohra (Founder) and Rohit Bohra (Director, MCA).

1The four levels

The department is the dividing line. Everything above it is company-wide; everything inside it belongs to one department and is invisible to the others.

LevelWho they areWhat they can see
Founder Rajesh Bohra, Dharmesh Bohra Everyone, every department. Belongs to no department — they sit above the structure, not inside it.
Director Rohit Bohra runs MCA Their own department only. Rohit sees the 10 people in MCA and nobody else.
Manager Anita Sharma and others, inside MCA Only the team picked for them. Hours and attendance — never anyone's daily logs.
Staff Full-Time, Part-Time, Intern Only themselves.
The one rule that explains everything else A Director's power comes from the department written on their profile. Change that field and you change everything they can reach — which is exactly why only a Founder is allowed to touch it.

5How Rajesh sets up MCA under Rohit

Every step below is done by Rajesh, from Team → Manage on the person named. Rohit cannot do any of them.

  1. Make Rohit the Director of MCA. Open Rohit's card. Set Role → Director, then set Department → MCA and Save. Team → Rohit Bohra → Manage → Role, Department
  2. Appoint the Managers. On Anita Sharma's card set Role → Manager and Department to head → MCA. Repeat for Salma, Shruti and Vaibhavi. Team → Anita Sharma → Manage → Role
  3. Give each Manager their team. Still on Anita's card, the “Team in MCA” box lists every MCA member. Tick the staff who answer to her — say Deepika and Jyotsna — and Save team. Team → Anita Sharma → Manage → Team in MCA
  4. Give Rohit his direct reports. On Rohit's card the “Direct reports” box lists MCA split into Managers and Staff. Tick the four Managers, plus any staff member who answers to Rohit personally. Save direct reports. Team → Rohit Bohra → Manage → Direct reports

6Rules the system enforces on its own

These need no policing — the system will refuse the change or clean up after you.

If this happens……the system does this
Rajesh tries to make Pranita (GST) report to Rohit (MCA)Refuses. A report must be in their Director's department.
Rajesh tries to make Aditya (Director) report to Rohit (Director)Refuses. A Director never reports to another Director.
Rajesh moves Jyotsna from MCA to AuditClears her manager and director links — they belonged to MCA.
Rohit is moved to another department, or is no longer a DirectorEveryone reporting to him is detached automatically.
Rohit tries to edit someone in AuditRefuses — and he could not see them in the first place.
Anyone tries to edit or offboard a FounderRefuses. A Founder's account can only be edited by that same Founder.

7Quick reference

QuestionAnswer
Can one department have two Directors?Yes — Audit has Aditya and Dilip. One is the normal case.
Can a Director run two departments?No. One Director, one department.
Who covers a department with no Director?The Founders, and only them. General is in that state now.
Do the Founders belong to a department?No. They sit outside the structure and see everything.
Does assigning a direct report grant any new access?No. Rohit could already see all of MCA. It records who answers to whom.
Are tasks and holidays split by department?No — those stay company-wide. Only people are separated.
